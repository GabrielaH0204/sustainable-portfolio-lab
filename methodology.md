# Methodology and academic notes

## Research question
Can a small sustainable portfolio combine diversification, historical risk/return analysis and explicit sustainability criteria without treating an ESG label as proof of positive impact?

## Portfolio structure
- ESGU: US ESG-aware equity core.
- ESGD: developed markets outside the US/Canada with ESG-aware screening.
- ICLN: global clean-energy theme.
- TAN: solar-energy theme.
- BGRN: investment-grade USD green bonds.

## Why the model is useful
The project separates market data, financial analytics, sustainability metadata and interpretation. This is important because an ESG label is not the same thing as verified real-world impact.

## Limitations
- ESG methodologies differ across providers.
- Thematic funds can be highly concentrated and volatile.
- Green bonds still carry credit and interest-rate risk.
- The model ignores taxes, transaction costs, bid/ask spreads and periodic rebalancing.
- Historical performance does not predict future performance.
- Yahoo Finance is used for convenient public-market data retrieval, not as an audited fund-accounting source.
